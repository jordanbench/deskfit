# Product discovery: occasional sit-stand movement on a 24-inch-deep renter desk

- Run date: 2026-08-24
- Guide/cluster: `/guides/standing-desk-vs-converter/`, small-apartment and first-apartment setup cluster
- Reader decision: Whether a renter can add occasional standing to an existing 24-inch-deep desk without replacing furniture, losing useful typing depth, or creating an unsafe lift-and-reset routine.
- Evidence limitations: Five exact U.S. Amazon pages currently report no featured offer. The sixth, FlexiSpot M7B, returned an internal retrieval error, so its current U.S. package and offer could not be inspected. Manufacturer and owner sources vary in freshness, and no candidate has Deskfit hands-on evidence. A dated CPSC no-match is not a safety certification.
