## Why this reader problem

Deskfit's shorter-user chair and footrest work now covers seat height, supported feet, and fixed-desk fit. The remaining live instruction is to bring the keyboard and mouse close enough to avoid forward reach, but a high fixed desktop can still force elevated shoulders after the chair and feet are corrected. This run starts with that measured reach problem, not a broad Amazon category, commission opportunity, or product quota.

The selection favors exact installed-height evidence, enough continuous mouse room, desk compatibility, removable installation where practical, and a mount whose load and depth match compact home-office furniture. Commercial value remains capped at 5 points and contributes no more than 2 points to any candidate here.

## Candidate decisions

### Kensington SmartFit Clamp-On Keyboard Drawer K55407WW — approve

- ASIN and exact variant: `B07V7MT63H`; black clamp-on keyboard and mouse drawer, model `K55407WW`, 32.4 x 13.3 x 2.4 inches, with 3.9-, 4.7-, and 5.5-inch SmartFit height settings.
- Canonical affiliate URL: `https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07V7MT63H/ref=nosim?tag=abbeybench-20`
- Decision slot: Default.
- Best for: A renter or home-office user with at least 32.4 inches of clear front-edge width and a flat top no more than 1.5 inches thick who needs a removable no-drill keyboard and mouse level.
- Skip if: The desk has a front apron, drawer, bevel, fragile finish, insufficient clamp land, or too little knee clearance for the chosen setting.
- Main tradeoff: The clamps and three settings make installation reversible and measurable, but the assembly is wide and exact-model owners report some typing tremble.
- Closest alternative: 3M AKT60LE for finer adjustment on an owner-controlled desktop that can safely accept a 17-inch screw-mounted track.
- Score: 82/100 — reader fit 24/25; evidence 18/20; longevity 12/15; differentiation 13/15; risk/return fit 10/15; demand 3/5; commercial 2/5.
- Hard gates: Exact ASIN/model verified; distinct default slot; no exact recall match; claims supportable; canonical affiliate URL compliant.
- Strengths: Amazon and current first-party material reconcile the part, variant, dimensions, clamp limit, and three height settings; installation is tool-free and removable; the settings can be checked against measured elbow height and knee clearance.
- Failure modes: Aprons, drawers, bevels, weak clamp surfaces, width limits, lost knee space, and some owner-reported typing tremble.
- Evidence:
  - Verified specification — [exact current U.S. Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07V7MT63H) maps the ASIN to black K55407WW and publishes the dimensions, clamp limit, and height settings.
  - Current first-party identity — [Kensington's official 2025-Q2 TAA list](https://www.kensington.com/siteassets/solution-pages/federal-page/taa-list-update-2025-q2_1744715202.pdf) still lists K55407WW as the SmartFit Clamp-On Keyboard Drawer.
  - Official product sheet — [mirrored 2024 Kensington K55407WW sheet](https://www.ergodirect.com/attachments/26511/Product-Sheet-Kensington-K55407WW-SmartFit-Clamp-On-Keyboard-Drawer-TAA.pdf) documents the exact part, UPC, clamps, sliding rail, and three settings.
  - Owner-reported pattern — [current exact-model Best Buy reviews](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/reviews/kensington-smartfit-clamp-on-keyboard-drawer-black/10732457) repeatedly support easy no-drill setup, removal, and sliding, with some tremble and setup cautions. Some reviews are labeled incentivized, so this is supporting evidence only.
  - Official safety check — [CPSC recall API search for Kensington](https://www.saferproducts.gov/RestWebServices/Recall?format=json&RecallTitle=Kensington) returned two unrelated products and no K55407WW or B07V7MT63H match.
- Decision reason: Approve. The exact current variant clears every evidence and hard gate and fills a real no-drill decision slot. Approval depends on publishing the width, clamp-surface, selected-drop, and knee-clearance checks. Commercial value contributes only 2 points.
