## Why this reader problem

The 2026-08-03 discovery run established that shorter users can need to raise the chair to meet a fixed desk, leaving their feet unsupported. The live chair guide correctly sends that user toward foot support, but the live footrest guide does not yet compare any footrests. This run starts from the resulting measurement problem—not from a broad Amazon category, price band, or commission opportunity.

The selection favors a documented height range, stable floor contact, and a footprint that can be checked against a compact desk bay. Rocking, massage texture, and commercial value are secondary. No product is treated as a medical intervention, and no comfort result is guaranteed.

## Candidate decisions

### Kensington SmartFit SoleMate Plus K52789WW — approve

- ASIN and exact variant: `B01NBWCTFD`; black SmartFit SoleMate Plus, one unit, model `K52789WW`, 21.9 x 14.2 x 3.5 inches and 6.6 pounds.
- Canonical affiliate URL: `https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01NBWCTFD/ref=nosim?tag=abbeybench-20`
- Decision slot: Default.
- Best for: A shorter user who needs roughly 3.5 to 5 inches of documented lift, foot-operated adjustment, and a heavier platform that is less likely to wander.
- Skip if: The desk bay cannot spare a 21.9-inch-wide by 14.2-inch-deep footprint or the footrest needs to move frequently.
- Main tradeoff: Strong stability and adjustment coverage use more under-desk space; owner reports say the height mechanism can feel stiff until its operation is understood.
- Closest alternative: Fellowes 48121 for a smaller modest-lift path, accepting fewer settings and more sliding/noise risk.
- Score: 82/100 — reader fit 24/25; evidence 18/20; longevity 13/15; differentiation 12/15; risk/return fit 10/15; demand 3/5; commercial 2/5.
- Hard gates: Exact ASIN and model verified; distinct default slot; no exact recall match; claims supportable; canonical affiliate URL compliant.
- Strengths: Amazon and the current first-party sheet agree on model, UPC, variant, dimensions, and weight; the range addresses the measured support gap; the rubber-bottomed six-pound platform is a credible stability feature.
- Failure modes: The footprint can crowd a narrow bay; setup can be confusing; the reader still must measure the needed lift.
- Evidence:
  - Verified specification — [exact current U.S. Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01NBWCTFD) maps the ASIN to black K52789WW.
  - Official specification — [2024 Kensington product sheet](https://az31609c.azureedge.net/literature/2099f501-84a4-466d-bfdc-34cfaa5a0996.pdf) documents the exact part number, UPC, dimensions, weight, materials, and warranty.
  - Owner-reported pattern — [current exact-model owner aggregation](https://www.testbericht.de/produkte/acco-kensington-kensington-solemate-plus-k52789ww) shows recurring stability, quiet-use, and adjustment positives with setup/mechanism complaints in the negative tail.
  - Official safety check — [CPSC recall API search for Kensington](https://www.saferproducts.gov/RestWebServices/Recall?format=json&RecallTitle=Kensington) returns unrelated products and no K52789WW, SoleMate, or ASIN match.
- Decision reason: Approve. The exact current variant is reconciled across four unique sources, the lift and footprint claims are measurable, the owner pattern exposes the setup tradeoff, and every hard gate passes. Commercial value contributes only 2 points.
